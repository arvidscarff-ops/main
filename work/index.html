Selected practice / 2019—now

Work

I tend to enter through one discipline and end up learning the whole system around it. These projects span brand building, physical production, events, content, marketing and software.

01

Agoos Apparel

Brand / Production / Operations

I started as Agoos’s head art director and designer, then later took over running the brand. That meant learning the whole operation: graphic and clothing design, organic social marketing, screen printing, manufacturing, e-commerce and customer support.

What began as a clothing brand gradually expanded into merchandise design and print work. One collaboration produced merchandise for Skogrejv ↗, a Stockholm rave collective.

  • Brand systems
  • Physical production
  • Organic distribution
  • E-commerce
  • Operations

02

Weekender

Product / APIs / Growth

Weekender began with a Berghs assignment: solve a problem by combining two APIs. I connected last-minute flight destinations with music festivals happening in the same place. A cheap flight is useful; a reason to go makes it interesting.

I kept building after the class exercise. Weekender is now a working public V1 and will continue to evolve. It is a discovery tool rather than a booking service, so live results still depend on provider coverage.

  • Product concept
  • API integration
  • Interface design
  • AI-assisted development

03

Noisey Neighbours

Events / Brand / Operations

A friend and I bought a far too large speaker system for his birthday party. Afterwards, we were left with roughly 800 kilos of speakers and no sensible use for them. Having spent the previous years around Stockholm’s rave scene, starting our own events felt like the natural answer.

The project became a multi-year collective. I co-led the branding, marketing, finance, logistics and live operation behind each night, while creating a stage for our own music.

  • Experience design
  • Project leadership
  • Audience building
  • Live operations
  • Team coordination

04

Personal brand consulting

Strategy / Coaching / X

For seven months, while living as a digital nomad in Southeast Asia, I coached people on building and monetising personal brands on X.

The work covered content strategy, content and copywriting, funnels, sales, AI systems and content marketing. Teaching the work forced me to turn instinct into methods other people could actually use.

  • Positioning
  • Offer design
  • Content systems
  • Funnels
  • Mentoring
05

Ghostwriting

Research / Voice / Conversion

The consulting work led to four months at a ghostwriting agency run by a major name in the “Money X” niche. I wrote for three clients: a salesperson with more than $1B in closed deals and two people from an LLM SEO agency.

I signed an NDA, so the names and exact account details stay private. During that period, the three client offers generated $200k+ in sales. My role was writing the X content used to promote them. I no longer sell ghostwriting as a service, but I still actively advise one client.

  • Domain research
  • Voice adaptation
  • Offer positioning
  • Conversion writing
  • Editorial consistency
06

Karnevalen campaign

Art direction / Content / Production

I pitched Karnevalen’s label, Luna Music, then built the artist duo’s brand bible and visual world before planning and producing a six-week release campaign.

We published 82 videos across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. The campaign passed 330,000 views, drove 4,494 profile visits and added 427 new listeners.

  • Client pitching
  • Art direction
  • Content strategy
  • Production
  • Campaign analysis
