Weekender

A cheap flight is good.
A reason to go is better.

Finding a spontaneous weekend away usually means jumping between flight searches, event calendars and maps. Weekender brings affordable return flights and nearby music festivals into one place.

The problem
The cheapest destination isn’t always the most interesting one. The useful connection is between a place you can afford to reach and something you want to do there.
The project
A discovery tool that connects flight options with festival dates and locations. An independent project spanning the idea, interface and implementation.
The boundary
A working prototype, not a booking service. Live results depend on provider availability and coverage; demo results are labelled. No audience or commercial outcomes are claimed.
