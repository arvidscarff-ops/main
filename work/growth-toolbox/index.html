01 / Data investigation

Above the law?

What does demand for accountability look like beyond a poll?

72% of surveyed US registered voters chose more investigations and prosecutions in the Epstein case in February 2026. The investigation places that finding alongside earlier recorded protest activity, with sources and downloadable data.

The protest records are from 2025 and mostly concern disclosure, not explicit prosecution. They cannot show whether the surveyed voters protested. The comparison raises a question; it does not measure a conversion from opinion to action.

03 / Scraping

Allabolag
scraper.

A JavaScript and Cheerio scraper that extracts company records from five Allabolag search pages for architects and writes CSV. The saved data was also converted to TXT.

The saved export contains 125 rows and 122 distinct identifier strings: 121 unmasked and one partly masked. These are not verified unique organisations. Three extra rows are exact duplicates; phone numbers are absent in 22 rows and addresses in seven. The original order, duplicates and empty cells are preserved.

The original dataset and actual scraper code are available as unchanged TXT files.
