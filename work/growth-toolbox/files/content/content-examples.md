# Getting the story out

## Sample journalist email

**Subject: Story idea: demand for Epstein accountability beyond the poll**

Hi [journalist’s name],

[Personalised opening]

In a February 2026 Navigator survey, 72% of US registered voters chose more investigations and prosecutions into people associated with Jeffrey Epstein and his criminal activities.

For a Berghs coursework project, I explored that finding alongside earlier US protest records. Our analysis found 491 event-days with Epstein-related demands in 2025, mostly about disclosure or cover-ups. Of those, 425 fell on 2 August, often within broader demonstrations.

That concentration suggests a question for further reporting: what helps turn calls for accountability into sustained public pressure? Interviews with organisers, alongside protest records from the same period as the poll, could help answer it.

The existing figures do not prove a gap between voters’ opinions and their actions: the protest records predate the poll and mostly concern a different demand.

The short article, charts and source notes are here: [Above the law?](https://arvidscarff-ops.github.io/main/work/growth-toolbox/above-the-law/). Would this be useful for a follow-up on accountability or protest organising?

Best,
Arvid Scarff

## Sample LinkedIn post

72% of surveyed US registered voters chose more investigations and prosecutions in the Epstein case in February 2026.

What does that demand look like beyond a poll?

For my Berghs project, Above the law?, I explored the finding alongside earlier US protest records. Those records mostly concern disclosure and predate the poll, so they cannot show whether these voters took action.

Explore the charts, sources and questions worth investigating: [Above the law?](https://arvidscarff-ops.github.io/main/work/growth-toolbox/above-the-law/).

### Accompanying chart

The accompanying 72% chart card shows the full response split rather than placing a percentage beside an unrelated protest count. Alt text: “Navigator survey of 1,000 US registered voters, 19–23 February 2026. More prosecutions and investigations: 72%; enough accountability / time to move on: 17%; unsure: 11%.”
