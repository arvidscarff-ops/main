Independent clothing project / Archive

Agoos
Apparel

Art direction, design & operations.

I joined Agoos and later took over running it. It was a small, hands-on clothing project rather than a conventional fashion company.

I designed collections and graphics, worked with garments and suppliers, and handled the shop and each release.

Scope Clothing · graphics · production · photography · e-commerce

01 / Design and production

Making
the clothes.

I screen-printed pieces myself, developed embroidery and sewn details, photographed releases, managed the shop and social content, and worked with manufacturers.

Each design had to work on a real garment and be possible to produce at our scale.

02 / Collaboration

Skogrejv
merchandise.

We also made merchandise with artists, businesses and cultural organisers. This collection became Skogrejv’s merchandise for a period.

03 / Operations

Running
Agoos.

I handled product ideas, sourcing, production conversations, e-commerce, photography, content and the day-to-day decisions behind each release.

Role

Art direction, clothing and graphic design, hands-on production, photography, e-commerce, content, supplier relationships and operations.
