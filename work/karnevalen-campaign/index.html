01 / Build the world

The campaign started before the first post.

The first job was to make Karnevalen recognisable. I developed a brand bible that connected the duo’s story, character, photography, video language, styling, content principles and graphic system.

That foundation made daily production faster without making every post look identical. The campaign could move between humour, trends, performance and storytelling while still feeling like one artist.

02 / Distribution and response

Attention was the problem. Content became the distribution system.

Karnevalen had music, experience and label investment behind it, but not enough attention. We published daily for six weeks across three platforms.
