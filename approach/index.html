Approach

Draft for reviewProposed language for this page — not yet a finished personal manifesto.

Start with the problem.
Make something useful.
Pay attention to what happens.

01

A problem before a product.

A solution starts with understanding what someone is trying to do, where they get stuck, and why the available options fall short. The format comes after that.

02

Make the idea tangible.

A working prototype makes an idea easier to question. It reveals assumptions that can stay hidden in a description, and gives people something real to respond to.

03

Clarity is part of the work.

How something is explained matters alongside how it functions. Design and marketing should help people understand its value without promising more than it delivers.

04

Leave room to change your mind.

Separate what is known from what is assumed. Use evidence to decide what deserves another iteration and what should be reconsidered.
